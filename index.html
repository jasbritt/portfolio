About

Bridging technical expertise and governance

I'm Jasmine Brittan — a Fulbright Scholar, engineer, and policy advocate from Derby, UK. I hold a First Class BEng (Hons) in Engineering from the University of Warwick and an MSc from New York University and the University of Leeds, with a dissertation published in the journal Climate Risk Management.

My career sits at the nexus of engineering and policy. As a Transport Planner at BuroHappold Engineering, I work on urban development and infrastructure planning. As the UK's Young Ambassador to the Commonwealth, I advocate for youth participation in governance across 56 nations.

Engineering Background

I've held research and engineering roles at MIT's JTL Urban Mobility Lab, NASA's Jet Propulsion Laboratory, the European Space Agency, Rolls-Royce, A*STAR Singapore, and the C2SMARTER Centre at NYU. At Rolls-Royce, I developed AI code to generate augmented images of nozzle guided vanes — work valued at £5 billion when projected for organisation-wide rollout. At ESA, I built and launched a sounding rocket at Andøya Space Centre in Norway, presenting findings at conferences in Barcelona and Biarritz.

Policy & Commonwealth

As the UK's Young Ambassador to the Commonwealth, I've represented British youth across the 56-nation Commonwealth — from Senior Officials Meetings at Marlborough House in London, to the Commonwealth Heads of Government Meeting in Samoa, to the Youth Consultation in Windhoek, Namibia, where I contributed to a declaration feeding directly into CHOGM 2026. I'm a founding member of Youth Council UK's Youth Advisory Group, the successor body to the British Youth Council.

Research Focus

My research focuses on the viability of new transport modalities, particularly urban air mobility and its environmental impact. My thesis at NYU/University of Leeds analysed the environmental benefits of urban air mobility across the United Kingdom, and was published in Climate Risk Management. I believe in using both technical skill and policy insight to widen access to opportunity for all.
